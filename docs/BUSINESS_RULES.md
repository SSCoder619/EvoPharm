# EvoPharm Retail ERP — Business Rule Specification

## Document purpose

This document is the authoritative business-rule specification for EvoPharm Retail ERP. It defines the business areas that require formal rule approval before implementation. Business Rules and Validation Rules are intentionally blank in Sprint 2 and must be completed through approved business decisions.

## Document governance

- **Owner:** Product and business stakeholders
- **Status:** Sprint 2 baseline
- **Change control:** Amendments require a dated decision, an identified owner, and impact review before development begins.
- **Interpretation:** Where this document is incomplete, no implementation assumption is authorized.
